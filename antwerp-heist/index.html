What we could not check

  • No verified timeline of the night. We found no source with a minute-by-minute account of 15-16 February 2003. The video shows a NO VERIFIED TIMELINE stamp for that reason.
  • How the vault door was opened. Unknown. The theories (a hidden camera, a picked concierge, copied or forged keys, an unscrambled combination) are his account, detectives' speculation or press reports; none is established in a court-reported finding we could read.
  • The escape route and the "lights off" claim. Both come only from Notarbartolo's 2009 Wired account, later disputed: police say they found the lights on, and detective De Bruycker (via CJR) says no detail of his story can be confirmed. We read the Wired text only in a reproduced copy and through secondary descriptions, not from the live original.
  • The sandwich and DNA detail. It comes from one family of sources (Wikipedia and popular retellings), not from a court document we could read.
  • Box counts disagree. About 160 boxes, 123 opened (2003 to 2005 reports) against 189 boxes, 109 opened (other accounts). The video says "more than a hundred".
  • Value. Estimates range from a court-era EUR 100 million up to several hundred million. Many boxes held undeclared goods, so nobody has a reliable total. The USD 500 million and USD 20 million (his claim) figures were not used.
  • The dump site of the bags. Reported near the E19 between Antwerp and Brussels, one blog says near Lummen; not confirmed, so the map shows no pin.
  • Other limits. The address of the Diamond Centre is disputed, so no building is labelled. "About two years" of preparation is used although two sources say four. We could not read the court judgment, Belgian Dutch-language press or Italian press beyond the 2023 interview page. The guard cut to one and Denise Oliver's description each rest on a single report.
  • Not named on purpose: no dealer, community or group is named or implied as a suspect. The insurance-plot theory has no evidence behind it.

This is a collage documentary. Documents, headlines, memos, cassettes and board cards shown on screen are reconstructions of the quoted text or our own summaries, not scans of originals. Vault, street and object photos and the 1947 and 1958 film clips are illustrations, not the 2003 crime scene. The video contains no photograph of the vault, the crew, a detective or a dealer; the only photograph of a real person is Leonardo Notarbartolo (Corriere della Sera, used for identification and commentary).
