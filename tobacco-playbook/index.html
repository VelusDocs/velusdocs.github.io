The Lie That Sold Cigarettes for 40 Years

Quoted statements in the video and where each can be read. Documents, memos and newspaper pages shown on screen are reconstructions of the quoted text. Quotes are given as they appear in the video; a cut is marked with an ellipsis.

Main sources and the short names used below

  • Brandt: Allan M. Brandt, The Cigarette Century: The Rise, Fall, and Deadly Persistence of the Product That Defined America (Basic Books, 2007). Source for most of the Plaza Hotel, Hill & Knowlton and research-committee material.
  • Frank Statement: "A Frank Statement to Cigarette Smokers", Tobacco Industry Research Committee, full-page ad of 4 January 1954. Transcription: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/A_Frank_Statement_to_Cigarette_Smokers
  • B&W 1969: Brown & Williamson, "Smoking and Health Proposal", 1969 (quoted as the epigraph of Brandt ch. 7; Truth Tobacco Industry Documents, UCSF: https://www.industrydocuments.ucsf.edu/tobacco/). Key documents: https://www.merchantsofdoubt.org/home/key-documents/
  • CTFK: Campaign for Tobacco-Free Kids, "Tobacco Company Quotes: Nicotine as a Drug" (internal documents with Bates numbers): http://tobaccopolicycenter.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/161.pdf
  • AJPH 2006: Gardner MN, Brandt AM. "The Doctors' Choice Is America's Choice: The Physician in US Cigarette Advertisements, 1930-1953." American Journal of Public Health 2006;96(2):222-232. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1470496/
  • Wiener 1996: Jon Wiener, "The Cigarette Papers", The Nation, 1 January 1996: https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/pages/frontline/smoke/readings/wienerarticle.html
  • Frontline: PBS Frontline, "Smoke in the Eye", chronology: https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/pages/frontline/smoke/cron.html
  • Corrective statements: text of the court-ordered statements, United States v. Philip Morris USA (2017): https://ash.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Tobacco-Industry-corrective-statements.pdf and US Department of Justice: https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/tobacco-companies-begin-issuing-court-ordered-statements-tobacco-racketeering-suit
  • NLM: US National Library of Medicine, "Smoking in America: 50 Years On": https://circulatingnow.nlm.nih.gov/2014/01/10/smoking-in-america-50-years-on/

This video is about the history of industry-funded research, not health advice. Smoking causes lung cancer, heart disease, emphysema and many other cancers, and nicotine is addictive (CDC, WHO). If you want to quit, talk to your doctor or your national quitline. Vintage cigarette ads appear as historical evidence, not endorsement.
