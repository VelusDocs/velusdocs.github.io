What we could not check

  • Fine amounts. The cartel's fines for bulbs lasting longer or shorter than the regulated life are often quoted in Swiss francs, but we never found the table. The video says so; only the direction ("longer or shorter") is sourced (Krajewski).
  • UK 1951 report. The wording of the Monopolies Commission report ("the best possible compromise", "misconceived") comes from a secondary source quoting it; we did not read the report scan. The video says "as the report is usually quoted".
  • HP chief executive at Davos. The quote comes from press reports; we found no transcript.
  • Epson. The case is the prosecutor's allegations. Epson denies them, is presumed innocent, and the 2 July 2026 hearing only set the date; pleadings are on 25 February 2027. "The first trial anywhere" is the campaigners' and Novethic's framing.
  • US states. The Apple settlement is reported as 34 states by Axios, but counts of 33 states plus Washington DC also appear; the narration says "more than thirty".
  • Founding date of the cartel. Sources disagree (see row 15); we say "the last days of December 1924".
  • Not used on purpose: nylon stockings, the "eternal bulb" suppression story, Swiss-franc fine amounts, market-share figures we could not verify.

This is a collage documentary. Documents, letters, memos, board tapes, cards and stamps shown on screen are reconstructions of the quoted text or our own summaries, not scans of originals. The Epson and Apple cases concern allegations and settlements, not proven findings of planned obsolescence. Archive films appear only as what General Electric told the public, never as footage of the cartel.
