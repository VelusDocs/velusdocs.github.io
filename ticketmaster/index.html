What we could not check

  • The verdict form. We did not read it. The wording of the findings is the New York Attorney General's summary, and all reports we read agree with it.
  • State count for the $1.72 finding. Sources differ (21 plus DC, or 22 plus DC). The video says "more than twenty".
  • Live Nation's arithmetic (257 venues, about a fifth of tickets, under $150 million before tripling) is the company's own reading; the $450 million on screen is labelled as that arithmetic "if tripled".
  • The Rapino-Trump contact. The video uses only the June 2026 filing as reported by CNN. On 21 October 2026 Live Nation filed an amended disclosure saying the February contact was a phone call in which the status of the DOJ lawsuit came up (with "no substantive terms regarding any potential settlement" discussed), and that President Trump "briefly appeared" at the 5 March meeting where the term sheet was signed but "did not participate in any substantive settlement discussions" (reported by Digital Music News and MLex, 22 October 2026: https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/10/22/live-nation-amends-trump-doj-settlement-disclosure/). No court has found improper influence.
  • The head of the antitrust division. Reports describe her exit as forced; the video says only that she "had left her post".
  • Varney's ten-year monitoring remark is reported speech from 2010 news text reproduced in a public comment, not a quotation.
  • Status of the court's rulings. As of 22 October 2026 we found no ruling on the settlement under the Tunney Act, none on Live Nation's motions to set aside the verdict, and no firm date for the remedies trial (reporting says "as early as February 2027"). These can change at any time; check the docket (S.D.N.Y. 1:24-cv-03973) for the current state.
  • Not used on purpose: the Kennedy Center / Ric Grenell allegations, the consumer class actions, the DC settlement, the UK CMA, and Slack messages shown at trial.

This is a collage documentary. Documents, quote cards, cassettes and boards shown on screen are reconstructions of the quoted text or our own summaries, not scans of originals. Allegations are marked as allegations; Live Nation denies the government's allegations and admits no wrongdoing in the settlement. The jury findings are those of the jury in the states' case; the DOJ's case ended in a proposed settlement.
