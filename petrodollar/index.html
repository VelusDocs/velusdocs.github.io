Every quote, number, date and document in the video, in the order it appears, and where each can be read. Documents, memos, quote cards, cassette cards and case-wall pins shown on screen are reconstructions of the quoted text; a cut is marked with an ellipsis. Where a claim rests on a single news report, the video names it ("according to Bloomberg", "according to Al Jazeera"), and so does this page. Current events are as of 12 October 2026. What we could not check, and how the video says it

  • The cable with King Faisal's "strictly secret" demand, the 1976 Treasury memo, the 1979 GAO text on the Treasury "blackout", the "add-on" mechanism, the four days in Jeddah and Parsky's role are known only from Bloomberg 2016. The video attributes each to Bloomberg in the same sentence.
  • No document we found says Saudi oil must be sold only for dollars. That is not proof that nothing was ever said, and the video says so.
  • The 8 June 1974 date of the joint commissions is given "by most accounts"; the text of the joint statement was not obtained.
  • Image notes: the satellite picture labelled "Khurais oil facility after a 2019 attack" (about 2:08) is a Planet Labs image of the Khurais facility dated 4 February 2017, not a picture of the 2019 attack. The photo labelled "Nixon, 1974" (about 8:45) was taken at Camp David in December 1971. The photo labelled "tanker traffic, Strait of Hormuz" (chapter 6) shows two U.S. Navy ships in the strait in 2012. None of these labels changes a fact in the narration.
